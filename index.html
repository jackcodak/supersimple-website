ABOUT

Hi, I'm Jack! I’m a Digital Marketer, Content Creator, and Creative Manager living in New Jersey. My mission
is to write, produce, and execute a creative campaign that premieres during the Super Bowl so my dad and
I can watch it. While we don’t know much about sports, we look forward to the ads every year.

To kick off this journey, I wrote and performed two original songs all about bagels for Hole In The Wall
Bagels, activated the ‘COMMchella Burger’ meal deal collaboration between #BeCOMM and 30 Burgers &
Brews, and co-founded the small production company, Jack & Kaliyah Productions.

Hole In The Wall Bagels

The client wanted to reinvigorate their image by catering to college students.
I wrote two music videos and co-directed the 'Breads & Threads' concert.
With that, I got the most rewarding compensation ever... a free bagel sandwich.

Music Videos

The brand was founded in 1996—a time of fisheye lenses & high saturation in video.
Inspired by hip-hop groups, my goal was to bring that video style into the 2020s.
'Hole In The Wall' and 'The Specialties' is available wherever you listen to music.

Breads & Threads

With the launch of 'The Specialties', we wanted to show the creativity of small businesses.
Partnering with RU Vintage, the event had four local bands, discounts, & chalk art.
60 attendees, 35% sales increase, & 19,000 views on Instagram successfully brought the client into the 2020s.

#BeCOMM

The client wanted to celebrate the Communication major at Rutgers University.
The #BeCOMM merch photoshoot consisted of Comm students along with interviewing students on #SpotlightSundays.
I'm most proud of activating the 30 Burgers & Brews collab to form the 'COMMchella Burger' & producing the COMMchella 2025 Ad.
